Subject: Draft Workflow – Complex Industry Gifts (PBM Lessons Learned)

Hi Ahsan,

Now that we've worked through the PBM Capital gift, I wanted to capture the process while it's still fresh. My thought is less about documenting PBM specifically and more about creating a repeatable workflow for future complex industry-supported research gifts.

Attached is a one-page process map that lays out the full framework: the 11-stage workflow, the two decision gates (funding pathway and complexity/escalation), the internal stakeholder chain, and the lessons learned from PBM translated into standing practice. It's a draft, but I think it captures the major decision points, internal partners, and takeaways well enough to work from.

My hope is that this eventually evolves into a CFR playbook that helps us navigate these situations consistently while creating a better experience for faculty, donors, and our internal partners.

A few of the biggest takeaways for me were:

- Lead with donor intent before discussing funding mechanisms.
- Educate partners on the differences between gifts, sponsored research, and contracts rather than steering them toward one option.
- Bring OSP, Gift Administration, Legal, and Finance into the conversation early when complexity exists.
- Use documentation (Statement of Work, memoranda, multi-party agreements) to clarify intent while preserving the philanthropic nature of the gift.
- Think of ourselves as quarterbacks — our job is to coordinate the right people at the right time while making the process feel seamless to the donor.

I'd love your thoughts on what's missing, what you'd change, and whether this could serve as the foundation for a more formal CFR workflow. If it's useful, I'd also like to eventually bring a polished version of this to Kelly, Heather, or NACRO as a best-practice reference rather than just an internal document.
